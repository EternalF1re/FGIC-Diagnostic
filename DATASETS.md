# Dataset preparation

The public runner consumes explicit development-pool manifests. It never scans an official test split during fold construction or checkpoint selection.

## Manifest contract

Every CSV must contain `path`, `label` and `sample_id`. Paths may be relative to the CSV. Labels must be contiguous, zero-based class indices. Sample IDs must be globally unique inside the development pool.

## Formal development pools

- CUB-200-2011: the 5,994 images marked as training by `train_test_split.txt`; the official 5,794-image test partition is excluded from OOF selection.
- Stanford Cars: the 8,144 official training images; the 8,041 official test images are excluded from OOF selection.
- Oxford Flowers-102: the union of the official training and validation partitions (2,040 images); the official test partition is excluded from OOF selection.
- Classify Leaves: the labeled training CSV used by the final controlled experiment.

The five held-out folds are generated by `StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)`. Pooled OOF metrics are recomputed after concatenating exactly one held-out prediction per development sample; they are not arithmetic means of fold metrics.

Dataset licenses and download terms are not granted by this repository. Users are responsible for obtaining each dataset from its official source.
